# Venkateswara Mills Landing

Create a clean, warm landing page for a traditional South Indian food powders shop called "Venkateswara Mills".

STYLE: Warm, earthy, traditional feel — use colors like terracotta, mustard yellow, deep red, and cream/off-white background. Include a subtle spice/food-themed visual touch (patterns or icons, not payment/e-commerce styling).

SECTIONS:

1. Hero section — "Venkateswara Mills", tagline like "Authentic Homemade Taste, Traditionally Made Since Generations", short intro line, and a background feel of spices/powders. Mention "15+ Years of Trusted Experience" as a highlight badge.

2. About section — Short paragraph about Venkateswara Mills being a trusted, home-style/traditional maker of pure, preservative-free spice powders with 15+ years of experience serving customers with authentic taste and quality.

3. Products section — Display as cards/grid, each with product name, short description, quantity, and price:

   - Idli Podi (Idli Powder) — ₹90 / 250g

   - Coriander Powder — ₹70 / 250g

   - Chilly Powder — ₹120 / 250g

   - Sambar Powder — ₹100 / 250g

4. Why Choose Us — 4 short points: 15+ years of experience, 100% natural with no preservatives, traditional homemade recipe, hygienically packed and fresh.

5. Contact/Order section — NO online payment or cart. Simple "To Order, Contact Us" block with:

   - Phone: 9884299130

   - WhatsApp: 9884299130

   - Address: 72, Madras Thiruvallur High Rd, 1st Floor, Padi, TMP Nagar, Mes Colony, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600050, India

   Add "Call Now" and "WhatsApp Us" buttons linking to tel:9884299130 and https://wa.me/919884299130 respectively. Include an embedded Google Map showing the location.

6. Footer — Shop name "Venkateswara Mills", address, contact info, and "© [year] Venkateswara Mills. All rights reserved."

IMPORTANT: Do not add any shopping cart, checkout, or online payment functionality — this is a display/catalog page only, orders are taken via phone/WhatsApp.

Make it mobile-responsive and simple to navigate.
